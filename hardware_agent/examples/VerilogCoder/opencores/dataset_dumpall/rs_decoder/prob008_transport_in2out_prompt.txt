## 4. Behavioral Specification

- **Idle State (Default):** 
  - The module waits for an `S_Ready` signal.
  - When `S_Ready` is asserted, the module initializes values: `RdAdd` to 204, `WrAdd` to 255, and sets both `RE` and `WE` to their inverted values. `state` transitions to 1, entering the operation state.

- **Operation State (1):**

  - On `cnt` high, the `WrAdd` increments. If `WrAdd` reaches 186, it resets `state` to 0 (idle) and sets `Wr_done` to 1.
  - On `cnt` low, the `RdAdd` decrements.

The module’s transitions and actions occur on the rising edge of `clk` or when `reset` is asserted.
